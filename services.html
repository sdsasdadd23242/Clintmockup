What is Vacuum Excavation?

Also known as Non-Destructive Digging (NDD), vacuum excavation is the safest way to expose underground assets—ideal near sensitive infrastructure like fibre optics or high-pressure gas lines.

How It Works

Using a high-pressure water jet to loosen soil and a powerful vacuum to extract it, this method is safe for all soil types and won't damage existing utilities.
